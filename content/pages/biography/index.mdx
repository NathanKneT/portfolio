# Full Stack Engineer & Visual Creator

I build reliable web products across frontend, backend and delivery. My core
toolkit includes **TypeScript, Next.js, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker and
CI/CD**, with additional experience in applied AI and document-retrieval
systems.

## Current work

I am a **Full Stack Engineer at Rokken**, where I contribute to medical-imaging
and applied-AI products. My work covers user-facing interfaces, APIs, secure
data workflows, automated testing, code review and software delivery.

The work is collaborative and multidisciplinary. I intentionally keep customer
and product-sensitive details private while sharing the engineering practices
and technologies that shape my experience.

## Previous experience

At **Capgemini**, I developed enterprise generative-AI and
document-retrieval applications with React, Python and FastAPI. I contributed
to a Flask-to-FastAPI migration, hybrid retrieval workflows and automated
Azure deployments with Docker, Terraform and CI/CD.

I later launched **DocsRetriever**, a B2B document-search SaaS, from concept to
production. It combined document processing, retrieval-augmented generation,
custom APIs and automated deployment. The product is no longer online, but the
experience strengthened my product judgment and ownership across the full
delivery lifecycle.

I also designed and delivered seven practical workshops on conversational
agents for Master’s students at Université Côte d’Azur. The
[course material](https://github.com/NathanKneT/Master-AI-Chatbot-Course-2024)
remains publicly available.

## Engineering and photography

Photography is not separate from how I think about software. It trains
observation, composition and restraint—the same qualities that help make
interfaces understandable and products coherent.

My visual work spans portrait, urban, night, live-performance and event
photography across France, Canada and Japan. This site deliberately gives both
practices space: engineering shows how I solve problems, while photography
shows how I see them.

- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, Node.js and NestJS
- **Data & AI:** PostgreSQL, MongoDB, RAG and generative AI
- **Delivery:** Docker, GitHub Actions, Terraform and Azure
