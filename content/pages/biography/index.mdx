# Full Stack Engineer and Photographer

I am a Full Stack Engineer based in Osaka. I work across frontend, backend and
delivery with **TypeScript, Next.js, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker and
CI/CD**, and I have practical experience with applied AI and document retrieval.

## Current work

I work as a **Full Stack Engineer at Rokken**, contributing to medical-imaging
and applied-AI software. My work covers user-facing interfaces, APIs, secure
data workflows, automated tests, code review and delivery pipelines.

I work with engineers and domain specialists while keeping customer and
product-sensitive information private.

## Previous experience

At **Capgemini**, I developed generative-AI and document-retrieval applications
with React, Python and FastAPI. My work included a Flask-to-FastAPI migration,
hybrid retrieval and automated Azure deployments with Docker, Terraform and
CI/CD.

I later built **DocsRetriever**, a B2B document-search SaaS, from initial design
through deployment. It combined document processing, retrieval-augmented
generation, custom APIs and automated delivery. The product is no longer
online; its [case study](/engineering/docs-retriever/) documents the system and
the decisions behind it.

I also designed and delivered seven practical workshops on conversational
agents for Master’s students at Université Côte d’Azur. The
[course material](https://github.com/NathanKneT/Master-AI-Chatbot-Course-2024)
remains publicly available.

## Engineering and photography

Photography has made me more attentive to composition, hierarchy and detail.
Those habits also influence how I approach interfaces and technical work.

My photographs cover portrait, urban, night, live-performance and event work
across France, Canada and Japan. The [engineering case studies](/dev-projects/)
explain how I build systems; the [photography archive](/projects/) documents my
visual work.
