<AboutIntro eyebrow="Nathan Rihet · Osaka, Japan" title="Full Stack Engineer and Photographer">

I’m Nathan, a Full Stack Engineer and photographer based in Osaka.

</AboutIntro>

<AboutSection id="journey" title="From France to Osaka, through Canada">

I studied at Université Côte d’Azur in France and finished a Master’s in
Applied Artificial Intelligence there in 2024. My first full-stack job was at
**Cawita Technologies** in Antibes, from 2022 to 2023.

I spent the 2023–2024 year in Canada, on a computer science exchange at
Université Laval with an AI specialization.

In April 2024 I joined **Capgemini** in Valbonne to build generative-AI
applications, first as an intern and then full time. I freelanced alongside
it, and went on freelancing until August 2025. In the same period I designed
and taught a workshop series on conversational agents for Master’s students at
Université Côte d’Azur.

Since September 2025 I work at **Rokken** in Osaka, on the web application
around its video-analysis pipelines. The <Link to="/engineering/">engineering page</Link>
shows how that work is built, and the <Link to="/resume/">resume</Link> has the dates and the
details of each role.

</AboutSection>

<AboutSection id="why" title="Why I build what I build">

I like the place where a web product meets heavy work behind it, such as GPU
pipelines and long-running jobs. That is my job at Rokken, and it is also why
most of my side projects are about real-time software.

Outside work I take games apart to see how they run. So far that gave two VR
mods for games that never had VR, and a port of a GameCube game to the PS Vita.
On each of them I test on the real device and keep a record of what changed.

</AboutSection>

<AboutSection id="photography" title="Photography">

My photography covers portraits, cities and live events across France, Canada

</AboutSection>
