# Full Stack Engineer and Photographer

I’m Nathan, a Full Stack Engineer and photographer based in Osaka.

## Current work

At **Rokken**, I build applications around computer-vision pipelines, from
video uploads and review interfaces to GPU job scheduling and background
workflows. I work mainly with TypeScript, Next.js, Python, FastAPI and
PostgreSQL.

## Previous experience

Before Rokken, I developed generative-AI applications at **Capgemini**, first
during an internship and then in a full-time role.

I also worked as a freelance developer from May 2024 to August 2025, initially
alongside my role at Capgemini. During that period, I built and launched
**DocsRetriever**, a B2B document-search SaaS, and continued its maintenance and
support through August 2025. The service is now offline; its
[case study](/engineering/docs-retriever/) documents the system and the
decisions behind it.

I also designed and taught seven practical workshops on conversational agents
to Master’s students at Université Côte d’Azur. The
[course materials](https://github.com/NathanKneT/Master-AI-Chatbot-Course-2024)
remain publicly available.

## Independent projects and photography

Outside work, I’m developing Kororo, a browser-based 3D puzzle game with a
[playable preview](https://nathanknet.itch.io/kororo-private-playtest) on
itch.io. My photography covers portraits, cities and live events across France,
Canada and Japan. The [engineering projects](/dev-projects/) explain how I
build systems; the [photography archive](/projects/) documents my visual work.

## Core stack

- **Frontend:** TypeScript, React and Next.js
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, Node.js and NestJS
- **Data & workflows:** PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Temporal
- **Interactive applications:** Three.js, React Three Fiber and Web Workers
- **Cloud & delivery:** AWS (Amazon S3, Amazon ECS), Azure, Docker, Terraform
  and GitHub Actions
- **Testing:** pytest, Cypress and Vitest
